A one-line comedy entry for the [Your AI Therapist's Diagnosis](https://make.fun/competitions/u-motasimfuad-your-ai-therapist-s-diagnosis-4be14a41) bounty on make.fun: the funniest single-line diagnosis an AI therapist hands its human, in 200 characters or less, judged head-to-head by a house judge model.

> Diagnosis: chronic main-character syndrome — you narrate your life to a chatbot at 3 a.m., then argue with the therapist you built.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) holds the entry line, validates it — non-empty, a single line, within the 200-character limit — and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` with mode `0644` so the grader can read it.
